# Autonomous Agentic AI Research Assistant

A fully autonomous AI research assistant that explores emerging scientific domains, generates research questions, acquires and cleans data, designs experiments, provides critical analysis, visualizes results, and allows PDF report downloads. Built with **Streamlit**, **LangGraph**, **Groq Llama**, and other AI tools.

---

## **Features**

- Input a **research topic** and let AI agents do:
  - Identify **emerging scientific domains**
  - Generate **research questions** (novelty & feasibility scores)
  - Acquire & clean **data**
  - Propose **experiment designs**
  - Provide **critical analysis** and recommendations
- **Visualizations & plots** of research metrics
- **Downloadable PDF report**
- Fully **autonomous AI pipeline** using agent-based workflow

---

- Streamlit
- LangChain / LangGraph
- Groq / OpenAI
